Meizu E3 A7A overlay v001
==========================

Baseline:
- Radxa official cubie-a7a-radxa-display-8hd/10fhd overlay topology.
- Existing Meizu E3 0.1.8 panel timing, reset, SEC touch, SGM37604A
  backlight and DSI flags that previously produced a working display.

Change:
- Add reg = <1> to dsi0_out: port@1.

Reason:
- This is the same OF graph correction made by Radxa commit
  4349013e00f21b703a75d620d8589f301ffc0493 for both official Cubie A7A
  display overlays.

Deployment:
- The package installs cubie-a7a-meizu-e3-display-v001.dtbo.disabled.
- It does not enable the overlay or edit extlinux.conf.
- Enable it through the Radxa rsetup/u-boot-update mechanism only after
  offline fdtoverlay validation.
- u-boot-update applies overlays to the normal l0 entry and intentionally
  omits them from the l0r rescue entry.

Not included:
- No HDMI disable overlay.
- No kernel DRM/DSI modification.
- No speculative timing, DSI flag, delay, GPIO or I2C topology change.
