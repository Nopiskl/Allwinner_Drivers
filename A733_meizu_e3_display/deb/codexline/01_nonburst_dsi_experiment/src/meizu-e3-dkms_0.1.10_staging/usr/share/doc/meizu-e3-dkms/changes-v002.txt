Meizu E3 A7A overlay v002
==========================

Baseline:
- v001, including Radxa's official dsi0 port@1 graph fix.
- Radxa's official Cubie A7A 8HD and 10FHD overlays, which both use
  MIPI_DSI_MODE_VIDEO without MIPI_DSI_MODE_VIDEO_BURST.

Single device-tree behavior change:
- Remove only MIPI_DSI_MODE_VIDEO_BURST from dsi,flags.

Reason:
- v001 completed panel initialization and displayed the LightDM greeter.
- Plasma/KScreen then applied an extended DSI + HDMI layout.
- The persistent journal recorded 116 sunxi_de_atomic_flush timeouts before
  DE0 started reading unmapped IOVA 0xfae77000 and then 0xfa000000.
- In this BSP, VIDEO_BURST selects multiple dedicated register paths and
  multiplies the DSI pixel/HS clock by 10/9. Both official A7A display
  overlays avoid that path.

Intentionally unchanged:
- MIPI_DSI_MODE_LPM and MIPI_DSI_MODE_NO_EOT_PACKET remain set.
- Meizu's native 1080x2160 timing and 157 MHz pixel clock remain unchanged.
- Panel reset GPIO/delays, supplies, SEC touch, SGM37604A backlight and
  GPIO-I2C topology remain unchanged.
- HDMI remains enabled.
- No kernel code is modified.

Deployment:
- The package installs cubie-a7a-meizu-e3-display-v002.dtbo.disabled.
- It does not enable the overlay or edit extlinux.conf.
- Enable it only through the Radxa rsetup/u-boot-update mechanism after
  offline fdtoverlay validation.
- The l0r rescue entry remains free of overlays.
